parser: the shared parser reads month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so 12/05 becomes 5 December. Reports come in by app, email and the booking-v2 form, from all tenants. This is the bug #4471 describes, and the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only reports sent through the form, and only from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it: the form has to print the tenant’s own order.

Intake tags disagree with this split in both directions; count by tenant and channel, not by intakeTag.

shift: dates move by one day

These are the reports the desk itself read as a one-day move. A day/month swap cannot do that: 12 May flips to 5 December, never to 11 May. The same meeting shows on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. This points at time zones (a date stored as midnight in one zone and shown in another), not at date order.
